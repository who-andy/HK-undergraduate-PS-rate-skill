# PS quality levels (experimental v1.0)

These are descriptive labels for the project's experimental **100-point score** (70 universal plus 30 programme fit). They are neither official Hong Kong university thresholds nor admission probability predictions.

| Inclusive total | Label | Operational interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 95–100 | Exceptional | Remarkably strong, well-evidenced independent reasoning, compelling academic alignment and exceptionally few substantive weaknesses. Reserve sparingly. |
| 90–94 | Excellent | Persuasive and specific evidence, deep reflection, strong programme understanding and only limited improvements needed. |
| 80–89 | Strong | Clear, well-developed argument and credible personal contributions, with identifiable room for deeper reflection, evidence or major connections. |
| 70–79 | Adequate | Reasonably complete and understandable but noticeably generic, insufficiently analytical or weakly aligned with the target programme in parts. |
| 60–69 | Weak | Some useful experiences, but insufficient reasoning, weak organization or weak major relevance substantially reduces persuasive impact. |
| 0–59 | Substantial Revision Needed | Fundamental evidence, argument, coherence or comprehension problems require substantial changes. |

## Required grading protocol

1. Assign integer criterion scores and compute 70/30 subtotals **before** choosing the corresponding inclusive band. Never reverse engineer a score from a desired label.
2. If programme research is unavailable, score only /70 and explicitly write **"100-point level not assessed"**. Do not rescale /70 into /100.
3. A 90+ judgement needs multiple well-supported high-quality dimensions; polished English alone is insufficient. For 95+, explicitly explain why important disciplinary and reasoning dimensions nearly reach their maxima.
4. Present factual inconsistencies, unverified claims, missed verified submission requirements and other material risks **separately**, even with a high score. Never infer fabrication from style.
5. Near-boundary changes (e.g. 89 vs 90) and small 1–3 point differences may reflect rater uncertainty. Discuss dimensions, exact evidence and disagreement when available, never treating bands as hard admission thresholds.
6. Do not invent university-specific score distributions, weights, thresholds, percentiles or applicant acceptance chances. This scale is an internal editorial instrument pending external calibration.
7. Language & Clarity stays 5/100 within the shared rubric. A supplementary language-only review can detail grammar, lexical precision, concision, cohesion, tone, but it must never be double counted.

Sample outputs:
- "92/100, Excellent under the experimental rubric. Professional alignment and reflection are well-supported; no inference about admission odds."
- "84/100, Strong. Better-supported critical reflection is the biggest potential gain."
- "76/100, Adequate. Substantive academic focus and personal evidence need improvement."
