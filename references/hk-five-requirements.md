# Hong Kong five universities: PS submission rules (2027 intake)

Source review: 2026-11-02. Structured companion: `requirements/hk-five-2027.json`.

This is a **route-scoped research baseline**, not a substitute for checking current admissions instructions. Intended for undergraduate international/non-HKDSE applicants including A-Level. Mainland Gao Kao, local Non-JUPAS, JUPAS, transfers and programme exceptions must not be silently mixed.

| University | What official sources establish | Limit | Choice handling |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| HKU | One PS explaining university and selected programme(s); exception for HKU-UCL Dual Degree Law | 1,000 **words** | One statement across programme choices |
| CUHK | One PS explaining selected programme(s); Medicine requires an additional separate statement | 6,000 **characters** for general PS; separate **250 words** Medicine PS | One general statement across choices |
| HKUST | A PS is included in required documents for international qualifications | No general word/character limit verified in reviewed source | Verify application portal and exact programme |
| CityUHK | FAQ specifies Applicant Statement **for each programme**, discussing university interest, aspirations and relevant experiences; local Non-JUPAS also lists a PS | No universal A-Level limit verified | Follow per-programme Applicant Statement fields; verify in application portal |
| PolyU | PS is **optional for most programmes**, with no general format; if submitted, explain suitability and upload as Additional Document | No general limit verified | Check programme-specific additional document requirements |

## Assessment implications

1. These pages describe submission rules and some selection context. **None of the cited pages establishes comparable internal PS grading weights across the five universities.** Keep the shared experimental 70/30 rubric, never portray it as a school's official rating.
2. Validate limits using the correct unit. **CUHK characters and HKU words differ.** Avoid converting 6,000 characters to an assumed word cap.
3. For HKU/CUHK, check authentic coverage of all programme choices sharing one statement. For CityUHK, inspect per-programme fields. For HKUST and PolyU, verify the form/portal rather than presuming shared statement rules.
4. PolyU optional for most programmes does not imply optional for all. A PS being required does not imply a known admissions weight.
5. This baseline is specific to the stated route/intake and can become stale. Recheck exact official programme and application-page requirements before giving a compliance verdict. If sources are inaccessible, label unconfirmed facts and do not fabricate limits.
